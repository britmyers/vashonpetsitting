About

A neighbor, not a service.

I'm Brit Myers, a Vashon Island resident offering pet sitting, house sitting, farm animal care, hive checks, and home care for island neighbors. I live here year-round — I know the roads, the rhythms, and the ferries. When you're away, I'm already nearby.

My professional background is in environmental science, research program management, and ecological systems, with experience across federal agencies, scientific networks, and conservation organizations. I co-lead The Estuary Salon, a small business just north of Burton.

I'm a former dog walker for the Seattle Animal Shelter and I have had experience with reactive dogs. I am comfortable working with animals that need a calm, patient approach. I'm consistent about communication — you'll hear from me when I arrive, when something is worth noting, and when I leave.

What I offer

Services

🐕
Pet Sitting — In-Home Visits
I come to your home to care for your pets on their own turf — feeding, fresh water, time and attention, and yard time or a short walk. Dogs, cats, small animals, and farm animals welcome. You get a check-in message with every visit.
Morning, mid-day, or evening. Multiple visits per day available.
🏠
Overnight House Sitting
I stay at your home overnight so your animals aren't alone and there's someone paying attention. Ideal for dogs who struggle with separation, multi-pet households, or anyone who wants eyes on the property. All regular pet care included.
Consecutive nights available for longer travel.
🌿
Plant Watering & Home Checks
For trips without animals — or as an add-on to pet care. Water plants on your schedule, collect mail, cycle lights, check for anything unexpected, and send a quick note. The small things that matter when you're gone a week or more.
Easily combined with any pet care arrangement.
🐓
Farm Animal Care
Feeding, watering, and checking on farm animals of all sizes while you’re away — from chickens, ducks, and rabbits to horses, goats, sheep, and pigs. Includes feed, fresh water, egg collection where applicable, and a welfare check to make sure everyone looks healthy and settled. Vashon has a lot of working animals; I’m comfortable across the range.
Pricing depends on the number and size of animals and the complexity of the setup. Can be combined with pet sitting or home checks. Let me know what your situation looks like.
🐝
Bee Hive Checks
If you keep bees and need someone to look in while you're away, I can do hive welfare checks — observing entrance activity, noting whether the hive is behaving normally, and reporting what I see. Best for neighbors who can walk me through their setup beforehand.
A trusted neighbor check-in, not a full inspection. Let's talk about your hive first.

Where I work

All of Vashon Island.
No ferry on your end.

I cover the full island — from the north end down to Burton and Portage, east to west. I don't commute here, which means I'm actually available when something comes up and not watching a ferry schedule.

I know the island vets and can get to the right one quickly if a situation calls for it. I'm comfortable with island realities — the ferries, the power outages, the patches of no cell service — because I live with them.

Not sure if you're in range? Reach out anyway. If it's on Vashon or Maury Island, I can almost certainly help.
